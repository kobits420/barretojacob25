# Credits

The pictures in Tem Tong are original animations written in `animations/trials.py` and rendered with Manim Community Edition. The rendered videos and stills live in `TemTong/Media/` and play inside the app.

Manim Community is licensed under the MIT License: https://github.com/ManimCommunity/manim

The app icon is a circle and a bar drawn for this project. It is not a downloaded image.

No photographs, stock images, or downloaded illustrations are used. The type on screen uses the iOS system fonts.

SwapTube (https://github.com/2swap/swaptube) was considered and not used. It requires a CUDA or HIP GPU to render. These pictures were made with Manim instead. The mark on the opening screen, the progress bars, and the icon are drawn in SwiftUI or as a simple geometric image.
